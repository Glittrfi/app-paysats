The insight

Selling your savings is the expensive option

Selling BTC for cash

  • ✕You permanently lose your stack, and all its future upside
  • ✕Triggers a taxable event every time
  • ✕You're forced to sell at whatever the price is today
  • ✕You have to start saving all over again

Borrowing against BTC

  • ✓Keep 100% of your Bitcoin and its upside
  • ✓No sale, no realized gain
  • ✓Get stablecoins now, repay anytime, no penalties
  • ✓Your savings keep compounding while you spend
